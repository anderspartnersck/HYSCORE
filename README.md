# HYSCORE

**Walk it in your browser: <https://anderspartnersck.github.io/HYSCORE/>**

HYSCORE is the house arcade off the 101 in the Valley, where every Castle Killscreen game has its cabinet. Park by
the MERC ZEBRA, go in through the front doors, and walk the floor the way you would in Myst: look around, click
where you want to go, and walk up to any machine to read its card. **PLAY** on a card opens that game in a new tab.

**Status:** a working build, not finished. The rooms and cabinets are 3D, rendered in Blender, and the walk is
pre-rendered: panoramas at each spot and the moves between them. The floor plan is a first pass.

## How to play

The logo plays first. Press any key, or click, at the attract and title screens to reach the menu, then pick
**ENTER THE ARCADE**.

- **Look around:** drag. The arrow keys turn too, and the mouse wheel zooms.
- **Walk:** click a green ring. Rings at the edge of the screen (‹ ›) point to ways you can't see yet, and ⌄ means
  behind you.
- **Inspect:** click a pink ring to walk up to a machine and open its card.
- **M** opens the map, where you can jump to any room. **Esc** pauses (resume, map, settings, back to the door, quit).
- **Settings:** sound on or off, and play or skip the cut scenes.

On a phone, turn it sideways and drag with one finger.

It saves nothing. Close the tab and HYSCORE forgets you.
